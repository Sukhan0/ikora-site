Ikora Player — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026
Developer: WaraSu, Ontario, Canada
Contact: support@ikora.site

This privacy policy explains how Ikora Player ("Ikora", "the app") handles information when you use it on Amazon Fire TV and other supported devices. Ikora is published by WaraSu ("we", "us").

Summary

  • Ikora is a media player only. It does not provide any content.
  • No account is needed. You never sign up with us.
  • Your source details and watch history are stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal information.
  • The Fire TV version contains no analytics, no tracking and no advertising SDK.
  • The app connects only to the playlist or server you add.

1. Ikora is a player only

Ikora does not provide, sell or include any channels, movies, shows or other content. You add your own playlist (M3U) or Xtream-compatible server from a provider you have rights to use. We do not run, control or endorse any provider, and we have no access to your provider account.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, Ikora saves the following locally on your device only:

  • Source details: the playlist address, or the server address, username and password you enter for your own provider.
  • Library data: the catalogue and guide data downloaded from your source, so the app can show and refresh your library.
  • Watch history and preferences: Continue watching positions, favorites and app settings.

This information is never sent to us. We cannot see it, and we do not keep a copy on any server.

3. Network connections

Ikora connects only to the playlist or server you add. It uses those connections to download your library and guide, refresh them once a day, and play the streams you choose. Your provider receives the requests your device makes, such as your login and your device's IP address, as with any streaming connection. Your provider's own privacy policy covers how it handles that information. Ikora uses a secure (HTTPS) connection whenever your provider supports one.

Ikora does not send any data to us or to third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer user accounts.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools in the Fire TV version.
  • We do not show ads in the Fire TV version and do not include an advertising SDK.
  • We do not use your device's Advertising ID.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information.

5. Purchases (future)

A Premium upgrade may be offered in a future version. If so, it will be sold only through Amazon In-App Purchasing. Amazon processes the payment, and we do not receive your payment card details. Amazon tells the app only whether you have the upgrade. Amazon's own privacy notice covers Amazon's handling of purchase information.

6. Changes for ads or purchases

If a future version adds advertising or new purchase features, we will update this policy before that version is released. We will explain what data is used and by which provider. Any advertising on Fire TV would use only the Amazon Advertising ID and would respect your device's interest-based ads opt-out setting.

7. Your control and deleting your data

Because all data stays on your device, you are in control:

  • Remove a source in the app to delete its saved details and library.
  • Clear the app's data under Settings → Applications → Manage Installed Applications → Ikora Player → Clear data, or uninstall the app. This deletes everything Ikora has stored.

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete or export on request. You can still contact us with any question.

8. Children

Ikora is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Security

Your information stays on your device and is protected by your device's security. Ikora never turns off certificate checks for secure connections. Keep your device and your provider login secure.

10. Your rights

We are based in Ontario, Canada, and we follow applicable privacy law, including Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Because we do not collect personal information, most access and correction requests do not apply. Contact us if you have any concern.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app adds new features. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
