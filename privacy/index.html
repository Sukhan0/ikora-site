Ikora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026
Developer: WaraSu, Canada
Contact: support@ikora.site

This policy explains how Ikora ("Ikora", "the app"), published by WaraSu ("we", "us"), handles information on phones and TVs.

Summary

  • Ikora is a player only. It does not provide any content.
  • No account is needed. You never sign up with us.
  • Your server details and playlists are stored only on your device.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • The TV app shows no ads.
  • Currently the app uses no analytics and no tracking.

1. Ikora is a player only

Ikora does not provide, sell or include any channels, movies, shows or other content. You bring your own provider: you add your own playlist or Xtream-compatible server from a source you have the right to use. We do not run, control or endorse any provider and have no access to your provider account.

2. Information stored on your device

Ikora saves the following on your device only:

  • Your server URL, username, password and playlist links.
  • Library data downloaded from your provider, so the app can show and refresh it.
  • Watch history, Continue watching positions, favorites and app settings.

This information is not sent to us. We cannot see it and keep no copy on any server.

3. Network connections

Streaming and library traffic goes to the provider you configure. Your provider receives the requests your device makes, such as your login and your device's IP address, as with any streaming connection. Your provider's own privacy policy covers how it handles that information.

To show posters and descriptions, the app may optionally look up artwork and metadata from third-party artwork services, only when needed. These requests contain the title being looked up and your device's IP address, and do not contain your provider login.

4. Analytics, tracking and ads

At this time, Ikora uses no analytics, no tracking tools and no crash-reporting that identifies you. The TV app shows no ads. We do not sell or rent personal information. This is true as of the date above. If it changes, we will update this page before the change is released.

5. Children

Ikora is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Deleting your data

All data stays on your device, so you control it. Remove a source in the app to delete its saved details and library. Clear the app's data in your device settings or uninstall the app to delete everything Ikora has stored. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete on request.

7. Security

Your information is protected by your device's security. Use a secure (HTTPS) connection when your provider supports it, and keep your device and provider login safe.

8. Your rights

We are based in Canada and follow applicable privacy law, including PIPEDA. Because we do not collect personal information, most access and correction requests do not apply. Contact us with any concern.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app adds features. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest change.
