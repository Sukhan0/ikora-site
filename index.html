Ikora is a clean, fast media player for phones and TVs.

Ikora is a player only. It provides no content. Bring your own provider: add your own playlist or Xtream-compatible server and Ikora plays it.

Ikora does not provide or sell content and is not affiliated with any provider. Use it only with content you have the right to access.

No Ikora account needed. Your sources and watch history are stored on your device.
