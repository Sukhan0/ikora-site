ONE WORLD. ONE STREAM.

Ikora is a clean, fast media player for Android TV, Amazon Fire TV and phones.

Ikora is a player only. It provides no content. Bring your own provider: add your own playlist or Xtream-compatible server and Ikora plays it.

No accounts, no tracking. Your sources and watch history stay on your device.
