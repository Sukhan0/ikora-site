What is Ikora?

Ikora is a media player for phones and TVs. It is a player only: it does not provide channels, movies or shows. You bring your own provider.

Add your provider

  1. Open Ikora and choose Add source.
  2. Pick Xtream or Playlist.
  3. For Xtream, enter the server URL, username and password from your provider. For a playlist, paste the playlist link (M3U).
  4. Save. The first import can take a while on a large library.

Quick fixes

  • Stream will not play: check that your subscription with the provider is active, then try again.
  • Buffering: try another network (for example mobile data or a different Wi-Fi), or a different DNS such as 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8. Restart your router if needed.
  • Provider unreachable: check the server URL, username and password, and confirm with your provider that the server is online.
  • Library is slow to load: the first import downloads your whole library and can take several minutes. Keep the app open until it finishes. Later refreshes are faster.

Content and provider issues

Ikora does not provide any channels or movies. Missing channels, wrong titles, expired subscriptions and stream problems on the provider's side must be taken up with your provider.

FAQ

Does Ikora include channels or movies?
No. You add your own provider.

Do I need an account?
No. Ikora has no accounts.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I remove a source?
Remove it in the app, or clear the app's data or uninstall to delete everything.

Does the TV app show ads?
No.
